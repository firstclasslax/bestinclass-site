FAQs

Straight answers.

Do I need to apply for every event?

No.

Summer Best In Class and our Committed events are application-based and invite-only.

Most of our fall showcases, winter showcases, camps, and clinics are open registration and filled on a first-come, first-served basis. Each event page clearly indicates whether an application is required.

How does the Summer Best In Class selection process work?

Applications open each July. Throughout the year our staff evaluates:

  • Your Sprocket player profile (sourced from your application)
  • Coach recommendations
  • Live evaluations at events

Invitations are sent on a rolling basis as rosters are finalized. Because roster needs differ by graduation year and position, players may receive invitations at different times throughout the year.

There is no official end date for applications; however, we recommend getting them in prior to October.

Can a coach nominate a player?

Yes.

Club, high school, and college coaches can nominate players they believe belong at Best In Class through our official nomination form.

Coach nominations are one part of our evaluation process, but all players are reviewed using the same standards.

Can I check my application status?

We do not provide individual application status updates.

If your player is selected, you'll receive an invitation by email through Sprocket. If you have not received an invitation, your application remains under consideration while roster spots continue to be finalized throughout the selection cycle.

View your Sprocket profile to confirm your application was submitted.

When will I hear back?

Invitations are sent on a rolling basis from summer through winter. We typically send an event capacity announcement in February announcing any remaining open spots.

There is no single announcement date. Some players receive invitations early, while others may be invited months later as evaluations continue and roster needs evolve.

In December, we will email all applicants still under review as we continue our process.

How do I update my application?

Please update your Sprocket player profile. Your application information feeds directly into your Sprocket profile, which is what our staff reviews throughout the evaluation process.

Please do not email updates to our staff.

I received an invitation. Am I registered?

Not yet.

Your spot is only secured after you complete registration and payment through Sprocket.

Receiving an invitation does not reserve a roster spot indefinitely. Once an event reaches capacity, registration closes.

Is there a waitlist?

If an event fills, qualified players may be invited from the waitlist as spots become available.

Because roster construction is based on graduation year, position, and overall event balance, openings are not always filled in chronological order.

For the Fall and Winter BIC, please join the waitlist through the event page. For the Summer BIC, the waitlist is tracked off event applicants.

What should my player bring?

Each event has a Player Information Packet that is emailed before the event with arrival times, required equipment, schedules, and additional details.

In general, expect to bring full equipment and water to every event.

Where can I find hotel information?

Hotel blocks and lodging recommendations are available on each event page when applicable, or see the on-site Hotels section.

Do you offer event insurance?

We offer program insurance for all First Class Lacrosse programs. Participants who wish to protect their registration investment can purchase coverage through our insurance partner: Vertical Insure.

We strongly recommend purchasing this insurance. Details about the insurance option will be provided in your program registration confirmation email.

Submit an insurance claim →

What's your refund policy?

First Class Lacrosse does not offer refunds unless First Class Lacrosse cancels the program. Please ensure you can commit to attending before registering.

Situations where refunds are not available:

  • Change of plans after registration
  • Player injury occurring before or during the program
  • Travel-related complications
  • Illness
How do I purchase event film?

Game film becomes available after the event and can be purchased through the event page once processing is complete.
